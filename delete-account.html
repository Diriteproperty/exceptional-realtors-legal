Delete Your Exceptional Realtors Account & Data

DIRITEPROPERTY INC. · Last updated: October 5, 2026

You can request deletion of your Exceptional Realtors account and associated data. Deletion is permanent.

How to request deletion

  1. Email us from the address on your account.
  2. Use the subject line Exceptional Realtors Account Deletion Request.
  3. Include your name and the email used in the App.

Diriteproperty@gmail.com

We process verified requests within 30 days.

On-device data

You can also clear App data or uninstall the App on your device to remove local storage (session, listings cache, and photos stored on that device). Uninstalling alone does not delete cloud records once cloud sync is enabled — email us for a full account deletion.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and profile (name and email) when we process your request.
  • Interest alerts and notifications tied to your user id.
  • Client-side local data for that account on devices you control (when you clear or uninstall).
  • Staff: owners/admins may also remove your staff record and related listings from the organization as part of operating the portal.

What we may keep

We do not retain your personal information after deletion, except where we are required to keep limited records to comply with legal, tax, or fraud-prevention obligations. Any such records are kept only as long as required by law and are then deleted. We never sell your data.
