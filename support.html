What is Exceptional Realtors?

Exceptional Realtors is a private mobile portal for brokerage staff and clients: manage listings and photos, invite admins and agents, track client interest, and access VIP promotional content. It is a tool for the Exceptional Realtors organization operated by DIRITEPROPERTY INC.

Frequently asked questions

How do I sign in?

Use the email and credentials provided by your Owner or Admin. Agents typically need an invite code as well. If you cannot sign in, email us from the address on your account.

Who can add listings and photos?

Owners, admins, and agents can manage listings according to their role. Clients browse and express interest; they do not publish brokerage inventory.

Which devices are supported?

The App runs on Android phones and tablets, and on iPhone/iPad where available.

How do I delete my account?

See Delete Account or email us from your account address. We process verified requests within 30 days.
