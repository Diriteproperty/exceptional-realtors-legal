Exceptional Realtors — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how DIRITEPROPERTY INC. ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects your information when you use the Exceptional Realtors mobile application (the "App"). We are based in Canada and are committed to handling your information in accordance with applicable privacy laws, including Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA).

By creating an account or using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Who we are

  • Company: DIRITEPROPERTY INC.
  • Country: Canada
  • Contact: Diriteproperty@gmail.com

2. What Exceptional Realtors does

Exceptional Realtors is a private real estate portal for owners, admins, agents, and clients. It helps staff manage listings (including MLS-style IDs and photos), invite team members, and receive interest alerts. Clients can browse projects and express interest. The App may show promotional content for Preferred or Standard offerings. It is an organizational and marketing tool for the brokerage — not a substitute for licensed real estate advice outside your relationship with the brokerage.

3. Information we collect

Information you provide

  • Account information: name, email address, and password or invite credentials used to sign in. Role (owner, admin, agent, client, or VIP tier) is assigned by the organization.
  • Team and invite data: staff names, emails, and invite codes created by owners or admins.
  • Listing information: property details, MLS-style identifiers, regions, notes, and photos staff upload for listings.
  • Interest and alerts: when a client expresses interest in a listing, we record that interest so staff can follow up.
  • Communications: information you send us when you contact support.

Information generated by the App

  • In-app notifications related to interest alerts and team activity.
  • Basic technical/usage data needed to operate the App (for example crash reports, error diagnostics, and performance data when something goes wrong).
  • Advertising or promotional identifiers when promotional ads are shown (see below).
In the current version, account, listing, photo, and alert data are stored primarily on your device (local storage). When cloud sync is enabled in a future update, the same categories of data may also be stored with our cloud provider so you can use the App across devices.

4. How we use your information

  • To create and secure your account and let you sign in.
  • To store and display listings, photos, team members, and interest alerts.
  • To show in-app promotional content relevant to your access tier (for example Preferred promotions for VIP clients).
  • To provide customer support and respond to your requests.
  • To maintain the security, integrity, and reliability of the App.

We do not sell your personal information.

5. Service providers we share information with

We use trusted third-party providers to operate the App. They process information only to provide their service to us:

  • Expo / EAS — app build, updates, and delivery.
  • Apple App Store / Google Play — app distribution.
  • Supabase (when cloud sync is enabled) — authentication, database, and secure photo storage.
  • Google AdMob (when ads are enabled) — may receive device advertising identifiers and limited technical data to serve promotional units. VIP and staff accounts may see fewer or different promotions.
  • Sentry (when enabled) — crash and error reporting. Diagnostic information (device type, app version, stack traces) may be sent to help us fix issues. Sentry does not receive your listing photos for advertising.

6. Photos and camera

Staff may grant camera or photo-library permission so listing photos can be added. Photos stay with the listing data on the device (and, when cloud sync is enabled, in secure storage tied to your organization). We do not use listing photos for third-party advertising networks.

7. International data transfers

Some of our service providers may process or store information on servers located outside Canada, including in the United States. Where information is transferred across borders, it remains subject to this policy and to appropriate safeguards. By using the App, you acknowledge that your information may be processed in other countries.

8. How long we keep your information (data retention)

  • On-device data: kept while the App is installed and your account is active on that device, unless you clear app data or delete content in the App.
  • Cloud data (when enabled): kept while your account is active, unless you or an authorized admin removes it.
  • Account deletion: you may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time (see our Delete Account page). We process verified requests within 30 days.

9. Security

We use industry-standard safeguards appropriate to how the App stores data, including encryption in transit for network features and access controls that restrict staff and client views by role. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

10. Your rights and choices

  • Access and correction: you can view and edit profile and listing details available to your role in the App.
  • Deletion: you can remove content you control in the App, or request full account deletion by email.
  • Requests: contact us at Diriteproperty@gmail.com to access, correct, or delete your personal information, subject to applicable law.

11. Children's privacy

Exceptional Realtors is intended for adults engaged in real estate activity and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, please contact us and we will delete it.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will revise the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, notify you in the App. Your continued use of the App after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
